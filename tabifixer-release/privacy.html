Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. If you have any questions, reach out directly at arthur@tabifixer.com.

01Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you use Tabi Fixer's services, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Travel dates and destination
  • Dietary restrictions, preferences, and special requests
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe; we do not store credit card details)

Inquiry Form

When you send the inquiry form, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, travel dates (or that they are undecided), number of days and travelers, cities you plan to visit, the plan you are interested in, your message, whether you would like a free 15-minute call, and any referral code you enter.

If you arrived through a link containing campaign or referral information (for example utm_source or ref), your browser keeps that information, the domain of the site that linked to us and the first page you opened, until you close the tab. It is sent to us only together with your inquiry; the rest of the link is not stored. We use it to see which channels and partners bring inquiries and to check referrals by hand. A referral code does not change the price.

Inquiries are stored in Google Workspace (Google Sheets), which only Tabi Fixer can access, and a notification of each inquiry is sent to our email (Gmail). Google may process this data on servers outside Japan. We do not add you to a mailing list and do not send automatic confirmation emails.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Website usage data — we currently do not use Google Analytics or similar analytics on this website. If we start using it, we will update this policy first.
  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information

02How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide personalized travel concierge services
  • Create custom itineraries and make reservations
  • Communicate with you about your trip
  • Respond to inquiries and provide support
  • Improve our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

03Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • Restaurants and service providers — your name, contact details, party size, and dietary restrictions may be shared when making reservations or arrangements on your behalf
  • Payment processors — Stripe processes payments securely; we do not store your card details
  • Service providers we use to run the business — Google Workspace (Google Sheets, Gmail and Google Drive) stores inquiries, our email and itineraries
  • Legal authorities — if required by applicable law or legal process

04Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it, as follows. Where the law requires a longer period, the legal period applies.

  • Inquiries that did not lead to a booking — deleted 12 months after we receive them.
  • Our messages with you during and about your trip, and your itinerary PDF and working notes — deleted 12 months after your trip ends. We communicate mainly by email (Gmail) and keep itineraries in Google Drive.
  • Booking, payment, refund and accounting records — kept, as a rule, for 7 years, or longer if the law requires it. Payments are processed by Stripe, which keeps its own records under its legal obligations; we see the card brand and last four digits, not the full card number.

Once a month, we check for records past these periods and delete them from our inquiry spreadsheet, our mailbox, Google Drive and any copies we exported.

Some places can’t be cleared at once or by us: an email trash folder (emptied automatically after about 30 days), a spreadsheet’s version history, our providers’ backups, and records held by Stripe. Information may remain there for a time after we delete it, so we don’t promise immediate or complete deletion from all of them.

06Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing — unsubscribe from any marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at arthur@tabifixer.com. We aim to send a first reply within 7 days. Before acting on a request we confirm that it comes from you, check whether any of the information must be kept (for example for a booking, payment or dispute), and then tell you what we deleted or kept and why.

07Cookies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our website. Cookies help us understand how visitors use our site so we can make improvements.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some features of our site may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

08Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

09Children's Privacy

Tabi Fixer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

For bookings already in progress at the time of any update, the policy in effect at the time your information was collected continues to apply.
