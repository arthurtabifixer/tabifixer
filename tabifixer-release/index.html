FAQ

Questions before you book

Something else? Ask me or email arthur@tabifixer.com.

What can you do in Japanese for me?

For example: contact places that only take inquiries in Japanese, request restaurant or activity reservations, check booking details and conditions, ask about changes or cancellations, and talk to a business for you when something goes wrong during your trip.

I help you communicate — the booking, contract and payment stay between you and the business, and they decide the outcome. I can’t guarantee that a booking, change, refund or special request will be accepted. If a request needs a licence or official authority, I’ll tell you if I can’t take it on.

Do you book flights, hotels or train tickets?

No. Tabi Fixer is not a registered travel agency, so I don’t book flights or accommodation. I can suggest options and explain how to book and buy tickets yourself.

What do “7 days” and “14 days” mean?

Basic covers up to 7 days, and Plus and VIP up to 14 days. That’s both the number of days your itinerary covers and the trip support period: up to that many consecutive calendar days from a start date we agree. I’ll put the start and end dates in my email before you pay.

Talking through your trip beforehand, making the itinerary and the reservation preparation we agree on don’t count toward those days.

How fast do you reply, and what if something goes wrong during my trip?

You can message me anytime. I’ll get back to you as soon as I can — I don’t keep fixed hours, response times vary, and immediate replies aren’t guaranteed.

VIP includes priority handling of your requests and phone assistance during your agreed travel dates. If I can’t answer your call, I’ll get back to you as soon as I can. Immediate responses are not guaranteed.

I’m not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 119 (ambulance/fire) or 110 (police) first.

What happens after I send the form?

Your inquiry is saved and you’ll see a reference number. I then reply by email with whether I can help, which plan fits, and the conditions. Sending the form is not a booking or a contract, and you only pay after you’ve agreed to the conditions. No automatic confirmation email is sent.

What if I need to cancel?

Before your consultation call, you get a full refund. After that, I refund the part I haven’t provided yet — based on the work already done (guide amounts are in the Terms of Service, and the amounts for your booking are confirmed by email before you pay). If I make a mistake or don’t provide something you paid for, I refund that part. Fees a business charges for a late cancellation or no-show are explained before I book and paid by you to the business, unless my mistake caused them. Refunds are made within 10 business days. If you bought before the current terms took effect, the terms in effect when you paid apply.

More questions (8)
How is this different from a tour guide?

I don’t walk around with you. I work behind the scenes — planning, handling calls and messages in Japanese, and helping when problems come up — so you can travel on your own with less stress.

Can you guarantee a table at a popular restaurant?

No — popular places fill up, and the restaurant decides. I contact them in Japanese and tell you honestly what’s possible. If a restaurant I booked for you cancels on its side, I’ll look for alternatives where I can, at no extra charge. I can’t guarantee that an alternative will accept the booking.

My trip is longer than my plan covers. What then?

Ask about an extension on the same plan. I’ll quote it based on the extra days, the itinerary and the help you need, before you pay. You won’t be moved to a higher plan automatically.

Do you only cover Osaka?

I’m based in Osaka and know Kansai (Osaka, Kyoto, Nara) best. I can also plan other parts of Japan — tell me where you’re going and I’ll be upfront about how much I can help.

What’s the difference between the free call and the 60-minute consultation?

The free 15-minute call is optional and for before you decide. The 60-minute consultation is included in every paid plan and is where we plan your trip in detail.

How do I pay?

After I confirm your request, I email you a secure Stripe payment link (card, US dollars). I don’t see or store your card details.

I have a referral code. Where do I put it?

Enter it in the form. It’s only used to know who referred you — it doesn’t change the price.

Can you help with visas?

I can share general information, but visas are your responsibility. For anything complicated, please ask an immigration professional.
