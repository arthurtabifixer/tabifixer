03Requests, SOS Messages & Changes

Plus includes 5 and VIP includes 10 Japanese-language assistance requests. One request is one purpose — for example booking one restaurant, or sorting out one problem with a business — including the usual calls and emails needed for it. Follow-up calls, confirmations and status checks on the same request don’t count as a new request. Requests beyond your plan’s number are quoted before I do them. How many SOS messages and changes your plan includes is stated in my email before you pay. No plan includes an unlimited number of requests.

If a business hasn’t replied within 72 hours of my request, I tell you where things stand. No reply on its own doesn’t mean the booking was declined; I keep following up within the same request, and we decide together whether to try somewhere else.

An SOS message is defined as a request for help with an urgent or unexpected situation during your trip — for example, a missed train, a booking problem, a sudden illness, or a language emergency.

SOS messages are not intended for general trip questions, additional planning requests, or restaurant recommendation changes. Those are covered under your plan's standard scope.

Unused SOS messages do not carry over to future trips or plans.

Changes to your itinerary. If I made a mistake, or missed something you told me beforehand, I fix it free of charge, as many times as needed. Changes you request yourself are included up to the number stated before you pay, under the conditions stated then.
